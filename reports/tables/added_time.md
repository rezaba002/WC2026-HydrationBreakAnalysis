## Second-half length

| tournament | source | matches | mean H1 added | mean H2 added | mean H2 end |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| WC2018 | exact half-end | 59 | +1.8' | +4.7' | 94.7' |
| WC2022 | exact half-end | 59 | +3.7' | +7.1' | 97.1' |
| WC2026 | shot-minute floor (lower bound) | 104 | n/a | +5.0' | 93.9' |

## Reading
- The rigorous comparison is 2018 vs 2022 (both exact): Qatar 2022 was the
  added-time anomaly the handoff flagged — second halves ran to **97.1'**
  on average (+7.1), vs **94.7'** (+4.7) in 2018.
- 2026 adds ~3 min per half **by rule** for the two breaks (they sit inside the
  clock), so 2026 matches are structurally longer than a no-break tournament.
- We CANNOT determine whether 2026's boards exceeded the anomalous 2022
  environment: exact 2026 board minutes are absent from every auditable source
  (the FIFA feed strips +X notation, 7/104 matches). The last-shot floor shows
  2026 second halves ran to **at least 93.9'** on average — the true
  whistle is later still — which only confirms they ran deep into stoppage, not
  whether they beat 2022. Reported as a floor, never as a board figure.
